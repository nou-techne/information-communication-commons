**Deploy checklist update:**

3. `cp -r app-src/dist/* app/`
4. `cp app/index.html app/404.html` (app-level)
5. `cp app/index.html 404.html` (root-level for GitHub Pages)

**React Rules of Hooks:** `useState` must be called at top of component, before any conditional returns (learned from white screen bug).

**Dimension counting semantics:**
- H/ counts participants (humans), not artifacts
- L/ counts total unique words from word_frequencies(), not artifact tags
- Other dimensions count artifacts tagged with hlamt:X
