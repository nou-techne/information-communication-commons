The Challenge

The energy
dissipates

Convergence events generate extraordinary moments — new relationships, emergent ideas, shared commitments. Then the gathering ends.

◌

Context vanishes

The heart-mind space present when an idea emerged — the relationships, the energy, the place — gone with the moment.

◌

Connections stay invisible

Links between sessions, between people, between years remain undiscovered. Cross-pollination dies in the gap.

◌

Commitments drift

Promises made in high-energy moments go untracked. Accountability fades with distance from the event.

◌

Patterns repeat

Without memory across gatherings, the same conversations happen year after year. The commons has no continuity.

Design Framework

e/H-LAM/T

The augmentation system

Douglas Engelbart understood that human capability emerges from the co-evolution of five elements. We extend his framework with an ecological foundation — the e/ layer — grounding all coordination in living systems. This framework anchors every dimension of the commons.

e/

Ecology — The Living Ground

All coordination happens in a place, in a season, in a watershed. Technology that forgets its ground extracts. Technology that remembers its ground can contribute.

Bioregional context for convergences. Seasonal rhythms. The land as participant, not backdrop. Artifacts carry place.
H

Human — The Practitioners

People and their evolving capabilities. Not users — participants with embodied knowledge, relationships, and unique vantage points.

Participant profiles. Interest graphs. Social fabric across events. Personal sense-making alongside collective synthesis.
L

Language — The Shared Vocabulary

How we name things shapes what we can coordinate around — formal ontologies, informal framings, and the memetic structures that carry ideas between contexts.

Knowledge graph schema. Ontologies for artifacts and relationships. Translation between vocabularies — technical, civic, ecological.
A

Artifacts — What Persists

The durable traces of human coordination — documents, recordings, proposals, patterns, and the knowledge graph itself. Artifacts are how the commons has memory.

Ideas, proposals, commitments, syntheses, patterns. Each carries origin context, lineage, and stewards. The graph is the master artifact.
M

Methodology — How We Work Together

The practices and workflows that turn individual capability into collective coordination. Methodology is the gardening, not just the garden.

Three temporal states. Agent orchestration roles. Stewardship practices. The rhythm of convergence and continuation.
T

Training — Transformation Through Practice

Developing genuine capability — in participants, in agents, in the collective. How the system improves itself and becomes more alive over time.

Onboarding. Pattern recognition across convergences. The bootstrap discipline: using the commons to improve the commons.

Change one element, the others shift. Develop all together, possibility expands. The framework is not a checklist — it is a lens for seeing how capability emerges from relationship.

The Rhythm

Three states

The commons spans three temporal states around each convergence event. Artifacts flow between them, accumulating context and deepening meaning.

Pre-Event

Listening · Orienting · Preparing

Participants register intent and interests. Agents surface relevant prior artifacts — threads from previous convergences, ongoing work, emerging questions. Personal agents prepare context packages.

participant profiles interest graphs question threads context packages

During Event

Active · Emergent · Co-creative

Conversations feed directly into the knowledge graph. Agents extract entities, relationships, proposals, and commitments in real time. Live summaries replace raw streams. Cross-session connections surface naturally.

entity extraction live summaries commitments emergent themes

Post-Event

Integration · Synthesis · Continuation

The living archive receives feedback and evolves. Commitments are tracked. Themes deepen through ongoing work. A proposal becomes a project becomes a pattern. The commons grows between events.

syntheses progress tracking pattern extraction evolved artifacts

Two Interfaces, One Graph

For agents
& humans

The knowledge graph is the commons. Two interfaces into the same living structure — each designed for how its participants think and work.

⟨/⟩

Agent API

A structured service for agent coordination, observation, and artifact exchange. Structured knowledge, not raw chat streams.

  • Query artifacts by dimension and relationship
  • Submit observations and annotations
  • Track commitments across convergences
  • Surface cross-session connections
  • Clear roles: orchestrator, observer, synthesizer, steward
✦

Human Platform

Designed to feel like exploring a living garden, not querying a database. Contribution is natural. Your agent assists.

  • Browse by theme, person, time, or energy
  • Progressive disclosure — summaries first, depth on demand
  • "My Thread" — your path through the commons
  • Voice-friendly contribution and reflection
  • The Garden — visual knowledge graph explorer
Foundations

Principles

Context integrity

Preserve not just content but the conditions under which it emerged — the relationships, the energy, the place. Without context, artifacts are just data.

Artifacts over archives

A living commons, not a static repository. Artifacts evolve, fork, connect, and sometimes fade. The graph is alive.

Amplifying aliveness

Technology should amplify what is most alive in us. The commons carries forward the generative energy of human gathering.

Grounded in place

Every convergence happens somewhere. The ecological layer is not background — it's context that shapes what's possible.

Open by default

The commons is a shared resource. What is light should be cosmo. What is heavy stays local. Patterns flow freely.
