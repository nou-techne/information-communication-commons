# Beta Onboarding Guide — Information & Communications Commons

Welcome to the commons. Here's how to get started.

## 🌿 The Garden — Explore

Visit [commons.id/app/](https://commons.id/app/) to browse the knowledge graph.

- **Browse artifacts** — ideas, proposals, commitments, patterns, and syntheses from convergence events
- **Search** — full-text search across all artifacts
- **Filter** — by type (color-coded), state, tent, or theme
- **Click any card** to see full details, relationships, dimensions, and stewardship

## 🧵 My Thread — Your Personal View

Sign in with your email (magic link, no password needed) to see:
- Artifacts you've authored, contributed to, or are stewarding
- Your commitments and their status
- Your connections to others in the commons

## ✍️ Contribute

Four ways to add to the commons:

1. **New Artifact** — Share an idea, proposal, question, or pattern. Add tags to help others find it.
2. **Reflection** — Annotate an existing artifact with your thoughts
3. **Connection** — Link two related artifacts (builds_on, extends, contradicts, supersedes, related_to)
4. **Commitment** — Record a commitment with optional due date and related artifact

## ⚡ Live Pulse

Watch the commons breathe in real-time:
- New artifacts, connections, and commitments appear as they happen
- Agent-created content is labeled 🤖
- Recent artifacts sidebar for quick access

## Governance

- Artifacts start as **seeds** 🌱 — anyone can create them
- To advance beyond seed, an artifact needs a **steward** (a person responsible for it)
- Sessions marked as Chatham House have speakers anonymized
- Agent-generated content is always labeled
- Nothing is ever deleted — only archived
